# Handing the archive to the researcher

`CONVERSION_REPORT.md` records what happened, for an auditor. The researcher knows things no check reaches, so walk them through these in order, with the file or command for each. Record every answer through the CLI. Change nothing on their behalf.

1. **Are these organisms plausible here?** Open `resolved_taxonomy.tsv` (or the published `occurrence.txt` taxa) together. A register match can be confident, valid and wrong: a plausible string for an organism implausible at this location. Only the researcher catches that. Trace anything implausible to its source lineage. If the resolution is at fault, correct the row in `confirmed/taxa.tsv`, with the evidence in its `provenance` column and their reply in `confirmed`, and build again.
2. **Do the answers recorded in their name say what they mean?** `answers.yaml` has the marker targets, the `controls` answers, and the metadata, each with its `provenance`. Paste the researcher's reply into each empty `confirmed`, fix any answer they correct, then build again.
3. **Do they endorse the adjudications?** `adjudications:` in `answers.yaml` lists each finding accepted, a validator issue or one that changed published values, with its `provenance` saying why. The researcher owns each one, and each term recorded under `term_states.impossible`: paste their reply into its `confirmed`.
4. **Did the right rows leave?** `proposed/control_roster.tsv`, with any `controls:` answers over it, says which samples were called controls and why. `excluded_rows_report.tsv` lists every removed row. The usual miss is a field site named like a control.
5. **The audit's findings.** They are in `CONVERSION_REPORT.md`, as disclosures, not questions. Confirm the researcher has read them.
6. **What is blank, and why.** `term_states.tsv`: every `Ask` row is a question addressed to them, with the evidence for why the tool could not fill it. Separate "absent from the source data" from "not yet done". The first is disclosed and final unless they supply the value. The second needs another pass.
7. **What the archive lacks.** `edna2dwc evaluate <archive_dir>`, with `--gbif` if the user agreed to the GBIF check, writes `EVALUATION.md`. Go through its failed and not-measured checks and missing terms together.
8. **Reproducibility.** `reproduce/` sits beside `dwca.zip` and holds the scripts, the answers and the raw inputs, so anyone with it can rebuild the archive. The researcher can share `dwca.zip` alone, or `reproduce/` too to let someone rebuild. From the conversion directory, `edna2dwc replay --check .` replays only `reproduce/` and compares with the `dwca.zip` beside it; inside `reproduce/`, `just check` does the same. Where the two are kept apart, run `just against=<dwca.zip> check`. The correct result is "No differences". A difference means a reader script, a package version or the installed `edna2dwc` changed, and `replay` prints the recorded versions beside the current ones.
